Delete your SpeakFolio account and data

You can delete your account and the data stored with it at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what may be retained. It follows the Privacy Policy.

SpeakFolio is made by Kentrologia Innovation Inc.

Delete in the app

  1. If you have a subscription, cancel it first in the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) subscriptions settings. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription.
  2. Open SpeakFolio and sign in.
  3. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. You can also start it from Settings > Privacy & Data.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

If you no longer have the app

Email privacy@speakfolio.app from the email address on your SpeakFolio account, with the subject Delete my account. We use the sending address to find your account, so a request from a different address cannot be matched.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account
  • Your profile
  • Your entries, including entries in Recently Deleted
  • Your voice recordings
  • Your transcripts
  • Your generated documents, including Salary Brief inputs
  • Your stored data-export files

When it happens

Deletion starts as soon as you confirm it in the app. For an email request, it starts once we have matched the request to your account.

Removal of files from storage is processed in the background. Scheduled workers remove queued files, and failed steps are retained and retried. Service failures may delay completion. A saved request is not a claim that every file has already been removed.

What may be retained

  • Payment records: kept by the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Stripe, depending on how you purchased. How long: kept only as long as required by law or for the provider's own billing and security records.
  • Analytics records: held by our analytics provider. How long: set by the provider's retention settings. Our Privacy Policy does not state a fixed period.
  • Service provider logs: service providers may keep request data for abuse monitoring or operations. How long: a limited time, under their own terms.
  • Backups: copies of your data in backups. How long: removed from backups when those backups expire.
  • Minimal deletion records: account identifiers, file paths, deletion status, the reason deletion was authorized, and recording dates. We use these records to retry failures and handle delayed uploads. They do not contain your audio, entry text, or Salary Brief inputs. How long: our Privacy Policy does not state a fixed period.
  • Legal obligations: anything we or our service providers are required by law to keep. How long: as long as the law requires.

The deletion process does not promise immediate erasure of records held by these providers. Deleting your account does not remove copies you have downloaded or shared.

For a request covering data held outside the application's database and file storage, or for help with any deletion request, contact privacy@speakfolio.app. Full details are in the Data Retention section of the Privacy Policy.
