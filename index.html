Questions you're probably asking

I've tried brag docs before. They never stick.
Brag docs fail because writing about yourself mid-flow feels awkward. SpeakFolio uses voice: 30 seconds of talking while the win is fresh, like you're telling a friend. No typing, no template to fill in.
I don't have time to document my work.
One short voice note when something goes well. Talk, don't type. The time you save comes back at review season, when the record is already written.
I don't want to sound like I'm bragging.
SpeakFolio writes your wins as professional documentation: what happened, what you did, what changed. You're not bragging; you're keeping receipts.
What do Free and Pro include?
Free stays free: unlimited voice recordings and 5 AI-generated outputs a month. Pro is US$9.99 a month or US$79.99 a year and adds unlimited outputs, PDF and Word export, advanced insights and career growth coaching. Pro is billed by the App Store or Google Play in your local price and renews until you cancel in your store's subscription settings. See the Terms.
Is my data private?
Your entries are yours. We don't sell your data, and your content is not used to train AI models. Data travels over encrypted connections. You can export your entries, and you can delete your account in the app, or by email if you no longer have it (see Delete account). Details are in the Privacy Policy.
Is there a web version?
Yes. Your entries are also available in the browser: sign in on the web with the same account you use in the app.
