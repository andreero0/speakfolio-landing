Introduction
Speakfolio ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.
Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information you provide when creating an account:

  • Name and email address (via third-party authentication services)
  • Profile information (optional)

Voice Recordings

  • Audio files are encrypted and stored securely on cloud storage services
  • Recordings are processed by us and our service providers, including third-party AI services, to provide Speakfolio
  • Automatic audio deletion is currently off. A choice of audio retention in Privacy & Data settings is coming in an app update. Until then, recordings are kept until you permanently delete an entry from Recently Deleted or request account deletion
  • Audio removal runs in the background through our file-deletion service; failed deletions are retained and retried

Usage Data

  • Device information (type, operating system version)
  • App usage analytics. From app version 1.1.4 on iOS and the matching Android update, analytics use a random account identifier only and never include your name or email address. Earlier app versions may have included them.
  • Analytics does not use precise or IP-based location
  • Error logs (for debugging purposes only)
  • Feature usage patterns, to improve the app experience
  • A notification token for your device, if you allow notifications, to send reminders
How We Use Your Information
  • Provide and maintain the Speakfolio service
  • Process your voice recordings and generate AI-powered outputs
  • Send you notifications (with your explicit permission)
  • Improve app performance and fix technical issues
  • Process payments through the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Stripe, depending on how you purchased
  • Analyze app usage to enhance features and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
Data Storage and Security
  • Authentication: Managed by our authentication provider
  • Database: Secure cloud services with row-level security policies
  • Storage: Encrypted cloud storage for audio files
  • AI Processing: Third-party AI services, used to provide Speakfolio
  • Transport: All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted service providers:

  • Authentication services: Account management and security
  • Cloud database providers: Data storage and hosting
  • Data processing services, including third-party AI services: Processing your audio recordings, transcripts and entry text to provide Speakfolio
  • Payment processors: Subscription management
  • Analytics providers: App usage analytics. From app version 1.1.4 on iOS and the matching Android update, analytics use a random account identifier only and never include your name or email address. Earlier app versions may have included them.

These services are bound by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.

What third-party AI services receive, and your choice about it, is in the AI Processing section below. You can request the current list of these service providers at privacy@speakfolio.app.

AI Processing

Speakfolio shares some of your content with third-party AI services to provide the app. You can allow or withdraw this in the app. Permission below explains how it works, including what happens for accounts that have not made a choice yet.

What is sent

  • Audio recordings
  • Transcripts
  • Entry text, such as titles, summaries, context, actions, results, impact and skills
  • Project names, success criteria and outcomes
  • Your career goal and target date
  • Document inputs, such as the target role, seniority and salary figures you enter

Why

  • To provide SpeakFolio features, such as your entries, documents and suggestions

Who receives it

To provide SpeakFolio, your recordings and text are processed by us and by trusted service providers acting on our behalf, including third-party AI services.

They may only use your content to provide the service. Their business terms, together with the settings we send with each request, do not allow your content to be used to train AI models.

You can request the current list of these service providers at privacy@speakfolio.app.

Where it is processed

Our service providers may process your content in the United States or other regions where they operate.

Training and retention

Your data is not used to train AI models.

This is separate from retention. Service providers may keep request data for a limited time for abuse monitoring or operations, under their own terms. Speakfolio stores your content as described in Data Retention below.

Permission

  • You can choose Allow AI processing in the app, and withdraw at any time, at Settings > Privacy > AI processing.
  • We are moving every account to this permission. Until that is finished, AI processing also runs for accounts that have not made a choice yet, including accounts created before this permission existed. This covers the features you use in the app and the scheduled weekly coach brief, career suggestions and project drift reasons.
  • If we cannot check your choice because of a temporary error, processing continues during this period. This is the only case where a withdrawal may not take effect right away.
  • When the move is finished, AI processing will run only for accounts that chose Allow AI processing. We will update this policy when that change happens.
  • If you withdraw, AI processing stops for your account, during this period and after it, apart from the temporary error case above. Transfers that already happened cannot be undone.
  • Content already stored in Speakfolio stays until you delete it.
Your Rights
  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data in Privacy & Data settings. See how to delete your account and data, including by email if you no longer have the app.
  • Export: Download your data in machine-readable format (JSON)
  • Opt Out: Contact privacy@speakfolio.app to request that we stop analytics processing associated with your account
  • Revoke: Withdraw microphone permissions via device settings
  • Withdraw AI permission: Turn off AI processing at Settings > Privacy > AI processing
  • Rectify: Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Object: Object to certain data processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@speakfolio.app

Data Retention
  • Account data: Account deletion removes your sign-in account, application records, recordings, generated documents (including Salary Brief inputs), and stored export files through a background deletion process. A saved request is not a claim that every file has already been removed. Failed steps remain available for retry.
  • Entries and transcriptions: Deleting an entry moves it to Recently Deleted. It stays there until you restore it or permanently delete it, and is then removed. Permanent deletion removes the entry and its versions and queues its audio for removal when no retained entry still needs that recording. Copies of entry content in generated documents or data exports follow those items' retention rules.
  • Voice recordings: Automatic audio retention is disabled during the app-version transition. Recordings are kept until you permanently delete their entries or your account. When automatic retention becomes available, an updated app can confirm a choice of 1 year, 6 months, 30 days, or Delete after processing. A confirmed choice applies to existing and future recordings only while the service has enabled that feature. Every choice requires successful processing or a completed no-speech result; retryable failures and unfinished processing are preserved. Defaults, previous unconfirmed settings, and sign-in migrations never authorize deletion. Entries and transcripts remain after audio retention. Before each new retention attempt, including delayed uploads and retries, we check that the feature is enabled and your current confirmed choice permits removal. Changing a setting cannot restore audio already removed or cancel a file removal already in progress.
  • Unlinked audio uploads: Scheduled cleanup checks tracked, unattached uploads older than 7 days. While automatic retention is disabled, this scan does not queue deletion. Once enabled, it requires a confirmed choice, completed processing, and no retained entry or active or retryable failed job that still needs the recording. Tracking stays until file removal succeeds. Unsaved uploads without entry or job tracking, including uploads cancelled by older app versions, remain until account deletion.
  • AI outputs: Generated documents, their saved inputs, and stored Word exports are removed through account deletion. Existing 90-day expiry dates do not authorize automatic deletion. The app does not currently offer individual document deletion or a document retention picker.
  • Data-export files: Download links expire after 7 days. Link expiry does not delete the stored ZIP or JSON file. These files, which may contain copies of entries and audio, are removed through account deletion.

File deletion is asynchronous. Scheduled workers remove queued objects from storage, and failed deletions are retained and retried. Service failures may delay completion. Contact privacy@speakfolio.app if you need help with a deletion request.

Minimal deletion records retain account identifiers, file paths, deletion status, the reason deletion was authorized, and recording dates needed to enforce your retention choice. We use these records to retry failures and handle delayed uploads. They do not contain your audio, entry text, or Salary Brief inputs.

Deleting data in Speakfolio does not remove copies you have downloaded or shared. Separate provider logs, backups, analytics records, and payment records are governed by the relevant provider's retention settings and legal obligations; the app's deletion process does not promise immediate erasure of those records. Contact us for a request covering data held outside the application's database and file storage.

Changes: We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We publish the updated policy here with a new "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of Speakfolio after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
